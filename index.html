The Origin & Vision

It all started when a dead, beat-up Like.Bike 2.0 was rescued for just $110. With nothing more than a piece of copper wire and pure garage determination, it was brought back to life. Out of the box, it features a dual-motor setup (2kW total / dual 1kW hubs) and a 52V 20Ah battery. Though it's currently battered and on its last legs, it still takes a beating and commutes every single day.

The Goal: Transform this survivor into an ultimate, all-weather, all-terrain beast capable of conquering any hill in any weather conditions and on any surface. As parts arrive and funding progresses, this page will be updated with regular photo logs, build updates, and rigorous video test reports.

A massive thank you to everyone supporting and following this build!
